# Attention kernels and attention-architecture variants for LLM inference (catalog, coverage to Oct 2026)

Scope: the "attention slot" of a plugin-based inference engine targeting one RTX 4090 (Ada, sm_89, 24 GB, no TMA/wgmma/tcgen05, FP8 mma.sync available), PCIe 3.0, KV cache in VRAM, MoE experts offloaded to CPU. Target models: gpt-oss-120b, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, Gemma 4 26B-A4B, GLM-5.3-Flash.

Source-quality legend used below: "[paper]" = primary arXiv/conference; "[repo]" = primary GitHub; "[2nd]" = secondary write-up or search-snippet summary (treat details as unverified). Several canonical arXiv links were supplied from prior knowledge of the literature (well-known IDs), not re-fetched in this session; numbers attributed to them are the papers' headline abstract claims.

Entry format: **Name** (date) — what it does | slot | HW preconditions | regime / gain | quality | maturity — sources.

---

## Q1. Prefill/decode attention kernels (exact softmax attention implementations)

### Takeaway
On sm_89 the usable dense-attention kernel family is the FlashAttention-2 generation (mma.sync + cp.async + ldmatrix): FA2, FlashInfer's FA2 templates, xFormers/cuDNN SDPA, llama.cpp's mma FA, Triton/FlexAttention, ThunderKittens' 4090 kernels, and SageAttention 1/2/2++ (INT8 QK / FP8 PV). Everything headlined in 2024-2026 for raw speed (FA3, FA4, FlashMLA, FlashKDA, cuDNN DSA indexer, SageAttention3 FP4) is Hopper- or Blackwell-only and must be re-implemented, not reused, on a 4090.

### Cited Findings

**FlashAttention family**
- **FlashAttention-1** (May 2022) — IO-aware tiled exact attention; never materializes the NxN score matrix in HBM; online softmax. Slot: prefill (also decode, poorly parallelized at batch 1). HW: any CUDA tensor-core GPU. Headline: 15% end-to-end BERT-large, 3x GPT-2 training speedup. Exact (no quality change). Mature/superseded — [paper arXiv 2205.14135](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14135); [repo Dao-AILab/flash-attention](https://github.com/Dao-AILab/flash-attention)
- **FlashAttention-2** (Jul 2023) — better work partitioning (parallelize over sequence, fewer non-matmul FLOPs, warp split of Q). ~2x over FA1, 50-73% of A100 peak. HW: Ampere/Ada/Hopper (sm80/86/89/90); this is the fastest upstream FA on consumer cards. Supports causal, sliding window (local), ALiBi, softcap, GQA/MQA, paged KV, varlen. Exact. Mature — [paper arXiv 2307.08691](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.08691); [repo](https://github.com/Dao-AILab/flash-attention)
- **FlashAttention-3** (Jul 2024) — Hopper-specific: warp-specialized producer/consumer with TMA, async wgmma, ping-pong scheduling, intra-warpgroup softmax/GEMM overlap, FP8 with incoherent processing. 1.5-2.0x over FA2 on H100 (up to ~740 TFLOPS FP16, ~1.2 PFLOPS FP8). HW: sm90 only — NOT usable on Ada. vLLM contributed attention-sink support into its FA3 build (used for gpt-oss) — [paper arXiv 2407.08608](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08608); [sinks note, HF kernels-community vllm-flash-attn3](https://huggingface.co/kernels-community/vllm-flash-attn3/commit/5d398ae29bae1f194cdfb90fffc197e779bad03a.diff)
- **FlashAttention-4** (paper 5 Mar 2026) — Blackwell co-design for "asymmetric hardware scaling" (tensor cores doubled; smem BW and exp units did not): larger tiles, redesigned async pipelines, software-emulated exp2 (polynomial on FMA units) to offload MUFU, TMEM + 2-CTA MMA to cut smem traffic in backward. Up to 1.3x over cuDNN 9.13 and 2.7x over Triton on B200 BF16, ~1613 TFLOPS (71% util). Written in CuTe-DSL (Python), 20-30x faster compile than C++ templates. HW: sm100 (tcgen05/TMEM) — NOT Ada. Ideas portable to Ada: software exp emulation to relieve MUFU (Ada's MUFU:tensor ratio is also a bottleneck at FP8/INT8 rates) — [paper arXiv 2603.05451](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.05451.pdf); [Tri Dao blog](https://tridao.me/blog/2026/flash4/); [Together blog](https://together.ai/blog/flashattention-4)
- **Hardware-Aware FP4 FlashAttention-4** (Sep 2026) — a separate follow-on paper building on FA4 with FP4; Blackwell-only — [arXiv 2609.04105](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.04105)
- **FlashAttention with FP8** — FA3 FP8 path (Hopper). On Ada, FP8 tensor cores exist (mma.sync e4m3/e5m2) but need CUDA >= 12.4; no upstream FA2 FP8 forward. SageAttention provides the practical Ada FP8 PV path (below) — [SageAttention README CUDA reqs](https://github.com/thu-ml/SageAttention)

**Decode-specific (split-K / sequence-parallel) kernels**
- **Flash-Decoding** (Oct 2023, Stanford CRFM/Together) — split the KV sequence across thread blocks, compute partial softmax per split, reduce with log-sum-exp rescaling; fixes FA's low occupancy at batch 1 / long context. Up to 8x faster generation for very long sequences. Slot: decode. HW: any. Exact. Mature; now standard in FA2 (`flash_attn_with_kvcache` num_splits), FlashInfer, vLLM, llama.cpp — [blog](https://crfm.stanford.edu/2023/10/12/flashdecoding.html)
- **FlashDecoding++** (Nov 2023) — asynchronized softmax with a unified (pre-estimated) max value to eliminate split-reduction sync; flat-GEMM optimization with double buffering for small-batch GEMMs; heuristic dataflow selection per shape. Reports up to 4.86x (NVIDIA) / 2.18x (AMD) vs HF and ~1.37x average vs prior SOTA. Unified-max can overflow if the estimate is wrong (needs fallback recompute). Research-grade, no widely used open repo — [paper arXiv 2311.01282](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.01282)
- **Lean Attention** (May 2024, Microsoft) — "stream-K"-style decomposition of decode attention: treats softmax rescaling as an associative reduction so work is split evenly across all SMs regardless of head count/context length (fixes FlashDecoding's load imbalance). Abstract reports avg ~2.6x over FA-2 and up to 8.33x at 512k context (verify against paper). Slot: decode, long context. HW: generic CUDA — [paper arXiv 2405.10480](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.10480v1)
- **PagedAttention v1/v2** (Sep 2023, vLLM) — KV cache in fixed-size blocks with a block table (virtual memory analogue); v1 = one thread block per (seq, head); v2 = additionally partitions long sequences (FlashDecoding-style split + reduce). vLLM reports 2-4x serving throughput vs FasterTransformer/Orca from reduced fragmentation. Slot: decode + KV memory manager. HW: any. Largely superseded inside vLLM by FA2/FlashInfer/Triton backends that natively read paged KV — [paper arXiv 2309.06180](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.06180); [repo vllm-project/vllm](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm)
- **XQA (TensorRT-LLM)** — specialized MQA/GQA generation-phase kernel that packs all query heads of a KV group into one MMA tile; also supports FP8 KV. HW: includes Ada. Closed-ish (in TRT-LLM) — [TensorRT-LLM gpt-attention docs](https://nvidia.github.io/TensorRT-LLM/advanced/gpt-attention.html)
- **POD-Attention** (Oct 2024, ASPLOS'25, Microsoft) — single kernel that co-schedules prefill (compute-bound) and decode (memory-bound) attention for hybrid/chunked-prefill batches on the same SMs. Up to 59% (mean 28%) attention speedup; up to 22% serving throughput with Sarathi-Serve. Motivation: prefill attention BW util often <5%, decode compute util <10%. Slot: mixed batch. HW: generic CUDA (evaluated on A100) — [paper arXiv 2410.18038](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.18038)

**Libraries / engines / DSLs**
- **FlashInfer** (paper Jan 2025, MLSys 2025) — attention engine for serving: unified block-sparse (BSR) "composable formats" representing paged, ragged, and prefix-shared KV; JIT-compiled customizable attention variants (user-defined logits/mask transforms: sinks, softcap, ALiBi, custom masks); load-balanced scheduler compatible with CUDA graphs; FA2 and FA3 templates. Reports 29-69% inter-token-latency reduction vs compiler backends, 28-30% long-context latency reduction, 13-17% for parallel generation. Integrated in SGLang, vLLM, MLC. HW: README lists Ada SM 8.9 (RTX 40) as supported, but "not all features supported on all compute capabilities" — [paper arXiv 2501.01005](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01005v1); [repo flashinfer-ai/flashinfer](https://github.com/flashinfer-ai/flashinfer); [2nd: Ada support summary](https://rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/flashinfer/en/latest/what-is-flashinfer.html)
- **Cascade attention / Hydragen (shared-prefix attention)** (Feb 2024) — split attention into shared-prefix part (computed once, batched as GEMM across sequences) and per-sequence suffix, merge with LSE. Hydragen reports up to 32x end-to-end throughput vs vLLM for large shared prefixes. Relevant to batch>1 / parallel sampling; low value at batch 1 — [FlashInfer cascade blog](https://flashinfer.ai/2024/02/02/cascade-inference.html); [Hydragen arXiv 2402.05099](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.05099)
- **xFormers memory_efficient_attention** (Meta) — CUTLASS-based FA-style kernels (plus FA2 dispatch), block-diagonal/varlen masks, split-K decode ("decoder" op). HW: Ampere/Ada. Mature but less active — [repo facebookresearch/xformers](https://github.com/facebookresearch/xformers)
- **FlexAttention / FlexDecoding** (Aug 2024; paper Dec 2024, PyTorch) — user writes `score_mod`/`mask_mod` Python functions; torch.compile generates fused Triton FA kernels with block-sparse masks (BlockMask) and a decoding variant. ~90% of FA2 fwd per PyTorch blog; covers sliding window, sinks, softcap, ALiBi, document masks, prefix-LM, tree masks, paged KV. HW: any Triton target incl. sm89. Excellent prototyping/agent target — [paper arXiv 2412.05496](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.05496); [PyTorch blog](https://pytorch.org/blog/flexattention/); [FlexAttention for inference blog](https://pytorch.org/blog/flexattention-for-inference/)
- **ThunderKittens** (Oct 2024, HazyResearch) — C++ tile DSL (16x16 register tiles) for writing FA-class kernels; matches cuBLAS and FA3 on H100 inference, 10-40% better on attention backward. Repo has 4090/Ampere ports. Good alternative authoring layer for Ada — [paper arXiv 2410.20399](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.20399); [repo HazyResearch/ThunderKittens](https://github.com/HazyResearch/ThunderKittens)
- **TileLang** (Apr 2025) — Pythonic tile DSL on TVM; examples for FA, FlashMLA-style MLA decode, linear attention, DSA/NSA sparse; targets NVIDIA (incl. Ampere/Ada) and AMD. fla added a TileLang backend for selected kernels (Apr 2026) — [paper arXiv 2504.17577](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.17577); [repo tile-ai/tilelang](https://github.com/tile-ai/tilelang); [fla README news](https://github.com/fla-org/flash-linear-attention)
- **llama.cpp / ggml CUDA FlashAttention** — several FA kernels (vector kernel for decode, tile kernel, mma/wmma kernel for prefill) with quantized KV cache (q8_0/q4_0 etc.), GQA packing, sinks (added for gpt-oss), sliding window. HW: Pascal+ (mma path Turing+). Relevant baseline for CPU-offload setups — [repo ggml-org/llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp)
- **cuDNN SDPA / Triton unified attention (vLLM)** — cuDNN fused flash attention (Ampere+); vLLM's Triton "unified attention" backend, which a third-party paper extended to express sink-anchored SWA masks that FA's window primitive could not — [2nd: Metronome arXiv 2607.02640](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.02640)

**Quantized attention**
- **SageAttention (v1)** (Oct 2024, ICLR 2025) — INT8 quantize Q,K (with K mean-smoothing to remove channel outliers), FP16 PV; Triton. ~2.1x over FA2 and 2.7x over xFormers on RTX 4090, near-lossless end-to-end — [paper arXiv 2410.02367](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02367); [repo thu-ml/SageAttention](https://github.com/thu-ml/SageAttention)
- **SageAttention2** (Nov 2024, ICML 2025) — per-thread (warp-granular) INT4/INT8 QK quantization plus Q smoothing, FP8 PV with two-level FP32 accumulation; CUDA kernels for Ampere/Ada/Hopper. ~3x over FA2, 4.5x over xFormers on RTX 4090 (paper claim). APIs `sageattn_qk_int8_pv_fp8_cuda` (Ada FP8 needs CUDA >= 12.4) and `sageattn_qk_int8_pv_fp16_cuda`. Supports causal, varlen, GQA; NO paged-KV mention, examples mostly video/image diffusion — needs work to become an LLM decode kernel — [paper arXiv 2411.10958](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10958); [repo README](https://github.com/thu-ml/SageAttention)
- **SageAttention2++** (code Jul 2025) — uses FP8 MMA with FP16 accumulation (`pv_accum_dtype=fp32+fp16`) for PV, exploiting the 2x faster FP16-accumulate path on consumer cards; README says requires CUDA >= 12.8 — [repo README](https://github.com/thu-ml/SageAttention)
- **SageAttention3** (May 2025; NeurIPS 2025 spotlight; v3 Jan 2026) — microscaling FP4 (NVFP4/MXFP4) attention; 1038 TOPS on RTX 5090, ~5x FA2 there; also 8-bit training attention (lossless fine-tune, slower pretrain convergence). HW: Blackwell FP4 tensor cores only — not Ada — [paper arXiv 2505.11594](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.11594v3)
- **SpargeAttn** (Feb 2025, ICML 2025) — training-free block-sparse + Sage quantized attention with online sparse-block prediction; Sparse SageAttention1/2 APIs released Jun 2025 — [paper arXiv 2502.18137](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.18137); [repo README](https://github.com/thu-ml/SageAttention)

**Feature-level fusions (all supported in FA2/FlashInfer/Flex on Ada)**
- **Attention sinks (learned per-head sink logit, gpt-oss style)** — adds a learned logit to the softmax denominator (no value); FA3-vLLM, FlashInfer variants, llama.cpp, fla (Apr 2026: "GPT-OSS-style attention sink support") implement it. StreamingLLM-style sinks (keep first-k tokens) are a mask/KV-retention policy instead — [gpt-oss model card arXiv 2508.10925](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10925); [fla README](https://github.com/fla-org/flash-linear-attention); [HF vllm-flash-attn3 diff](https://huggingface.co/kernels-community/vllm-flash-attn3/commit/5d398ae29bae1f194cdfb90fffc197e779bad03a.diff)
- **Logit softcapping** (Gemma 2) — tanh(s/c)*c on attention logits; FA2 supports softcap; Gemma 3 replaced softcapping with QK-norm — [Gemma 2 arXiv 2408.00118](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00118); [Gemma 3 arXiv 2503.19786](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19786)
- **ALiBi** — linear distance bias fused into score computation — [arXiv 2108.12409](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.12409); **RoPE fusion** — apply rotary to Q/K in the QKV-projection epilogue or attention prologue (FlashMLA now ships a fused norm+RoPE+attn+RoPE+cast kernel, Blackwell-only) — [RoPE arXiv 2104.09864](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.09864); [FlashMLA repo](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/FlashMLA)
- **GQA/MQA packing** — process all query heads sharing a KV head in one tile (M dimension = heads x query tokens) so decode becomes a small GEMM rather than GEMV; core trick in FA2 decode, FlashInfer, XQA, llama.cpp — [TRT-LLM docs](https://nvidia.github.io/TensorRT-LLM/advanced/gpt-attention.html); [FlashInfer paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01005v1)
- **Tree / speculative-decoding masks** — custom tree attention mask so many draft tokens verify in one pass (SpecInfer token trees; Medusa/EAGLE heads); supported via FlashInfer custom masks and FlexAttention mask_mod — [SpecInfer arXiv 2305.09781](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.09781); [Medusa arXiv 2401.10774](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10774)
- **Ring / Striped attention (context parallel)** — shard sequence across devices and rotate KV blocks in a ring overlapping comms; striped = cyclic token assignment to balance causal work. Multi-GPU only — irrelevant for one 4090 — [Ring arXiv 2310.01889](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01889); [Striped arXiv 2311.09431](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09431)
- **FlashMask** (Oct 2024) — column-wise sparse mask representation for FA, O(N) mask memory — [arXiv 2410.01359](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.01359)

### Inferences
- On a 4090, INT8 and FP8 tensor throughput is ~4x the FP16-with-FP32-accumulate rate on GeForce parts (Ada whitepaper rate table; GeForce halves FP32-accumulate tensor rate — [Ada whitepaper](https://images.nvidia.com/aem-dam/Solutions/geforce/ada/nvidia-ada-gpu-architecture.pdf)), which is why Sage-style INT8-QK/FP8-PV kernels give 2-3x on 4090 for compute-bound prefill. Decode is memory-bound so the win there comes only from smaller KV (FP8/INT8 KV), not from faster MMA.
- The agent's highest-value "attention prefill" search space on sm_89: tile shapes (BLOCK_M/N 64-128), num_warps, pipelining stages under the ~100 KB/block smem limit, FP16-accumulate vs FP32-accumulate PV, INT8 QK, exp2 emulation (FA4 trick) to relieve MUFU at INT8 rates.
- For decode: split-K count tuning vs context length, GQA head packing, FP8/INT8 KV dequant fused into the load, CUDA-graph-friendly fixed-shape scheduling (FlashInfer planner) matter more than MMA choice.
- FlexAttention is the cheapest way for an agent to get correct sinks+SWA (gpt-oss), SWA 1024/global (Gemma 4), and tree masks on Ada before hand-optimizing.

### Gaps
- No primary benchmark found for absolute FA2 TFLOPS on RTX 4090 or for FlashInfer FP8-KV + sinks on sm_89 specifically; FlashInfer feature-by-arch matrix not fetched.
- FlashDecoding++ code availability not verified. Lean Attention headline numbers recalled from abstract, not re-fetched.
- SageAttention 4090 numbers in README are images; paper claims used instead.

## Q2. Architecture variants that change the attention slot (dense-family, KV-shape, windowing, sinks)

### Takeaway
All four targets use KV-reduction variants: gpt-oss (GQA 8 KV heads, alternating SWA-128 + full, learned sinks), Qwen3.6 (GQA 2 KV heads, head_dim 256, sigmoid output gate, only 1 in 4 layers has KV), Gemma 4 (5:1 SWA-1024 : global, K=V on global layers), GLM-5.3-Flash (NoPE absorbed MLA + DSA indexer on 11 of 45 layers, KDA on 34). The attention slot therefore must be a family of kernels selected per layer, not one kernel.

### Cited Findings
- **MQA** (2019) — single shared K/V head; minimal KV, some quality loss — [arXiv 1911.02150](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.02150)
- **GQA** (2023) — G KV groups; near-MHA quality at near-MQA speed; uptraining from MHA checkpoints — [arXiv 2305.13245](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13245)
- **MLA (Multi-head Latent Attention)** (DeepSeek-V2, May 2024) — compress K/V into a low-rank latent (512) plus decoupled RoPE key (64); KV cache -93.3%, 5.76x max generation throughput vs DeepSeek 67B. **Absorption trick**: fold W_UK into W_Q and W_UV into W_O at decode so attention runs directly on the latent (MQA-like, head dim 576/512), compute-heavier but memory-light; for prefill, decompress and run normal MHA. Kernels: FlashMLA (DeepSeek; now SM100/SM103 + Ascend only — Hopper dropped 2026-09-30, older commit `ba89a34` retains sm90), FlashInfer MLA, TileLang MLA examples, vLLM/SGLang Triton MLA — [DeepSeek-V2 arXiv 2405.04434](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.04434); [FlashMLA repo](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/FlashMLA)
- **FlashMLA** (Feb 2025 →) — status Oct 2026: SM100/103 (CUDA 13.1+) and Ascend 950; sparse prefill (DSA), sparse decode requiring FP8/FP4 KV (no BF16 KV), fused norm+RoPE+attn+RoPE+cast, dense MHA fwd/bwd; B200 peaks 1460 TFLOPS fused prefill, 1024 TFLOPS sparse decode; DeepSeek-V4.1 kernels added 2026-09-10. Community ports to MetaX, Moore Threads, Hygon, Iluvatar, AMD (aiter). No Ada/Ampere support — [FlashMLA repo](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/FlashMLA)
- **TransMLA** (Feb 2025) — converts GQA checkpoints to MLA — [arXiv 2502.07864](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.07864)
- **Grouped-Tied / Grouped Latent Attention (GTA, GLA-latent)** (May 2025, Zadouri/Strauss/Dao) — decode-oriented variants that raise arithmetic intensity (tie K and V, group latents) to match MLA quality with better parallelism — [arXiv 2505.21487](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21487)
- **Tensor Product Attention / MFA** — factorized Q/K/V to shrink KV — [TPA arXiv 2501.06425](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.06425); [MFA arXiv 2412.19255](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.19255)
- **Sliding-window / local attention** — Longformer, Mistral 7B (W=4096 rolling buffer), Gemma 2/3 local:global interleave (Gemma 3: 5:1, window 1024). KV for window layers is bounded at W tokens (ring buffer) — [Longformer arXiv 2004.05150](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.05150); [Mistral arXiv 2310.06825](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06825); [Gemma 3 arXiv 2503.19786](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19786)
- **gpt-oss attention** (Aug 2025) — alternating banded window (128 tokens) and full dense layers, GQA, learned attention sinks per head — [gpt-oss model card arXiv 2508.10925](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10925)
- **Gemma 4 26B-A4B** (2026) — [2nd] 30 layers, 1024-token SWA, 5:1 local:global, last layer global, global layers GQA 8:1, K=V on global layers (value projection absent, K copied to V), 256K context, 128 experts top-8 + 1 shared; "shared KV caching" mentioned without detail; small models reportedly use window 512 — [aman.ai primer](https://aman.ai/primers/ai/gemma-4/); [Grootendorst visual guide](https://newsletter.maartengrootendorst.com/p/a-visual-guide-to-gemma-4); [NVIDIA Megatron Bridge Gemma4](https://docs.nvidia.com/nemo/megatron-bridge/0.5.0/apidocs/bridge/bridge.models.gemma.gemma4_bridge.html)
- **Qwen3.6-35B-A3B** (2026) — [2nd] 40 layers: 30 Gated DeltaNet + 10 full gated attention (indices 3,7,11,...); attention 16 Q heads / 2 KV heads / head_dim 256; Q projection doubled, second half = sigmoid output gate; GDN with causal conv1d k=4; 256 experts, 8 routed + 1 shared; 262K native context — [HF community blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/EXDai/qwen36-35b-a3b-architecture-overview); [vast.ai](https://vast.ai/model/qwen36-35b-a3b)
- **Gated attention (output gating)** (May 2025, Qwen) — elementwise sigmoid gate after SDPA; adds non-linearity, sparsity, removes attention-sink/massive activations; adopted in Qwen3-Next/3.5/3.6. Kernel impact: one fused epilogue multiply — [arXiv 2505.06708](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.06708)
- **GLM-5.3-Flash** (2026) — [2nd, consistent across PRs] ~321B total / 18B active, 45 layers: 34 KDA + 11 NoPE absorbed-MLA with DSA ("k-pool" indexer: index_kpool=4, always-select-tail, index_topk=2048), mHC 4-stream residual, 288 experts top-8, 1M context, 1 MTP layer; llama.cpp PR #27773 open for support — [Megatron-LM PR #7054](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/7054); [llama.cpp PR #27773](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/27773); [vLLM recipe](https://recipes.vllm.ai/zai-org/GLM-5.3-Flash); [Raschka on X](https://x.com/rasbt/status/2092629415813365899)
- **Attention sinks / StreamingLLM** (Sep 2023) — keep first ~4 tokens' KV + rolling window gives stable perplexity to 4M tokens; up to 22.2x vs sliding-window-with-recompute. gpt-oss bakes in a learned sink instead — [arXiv 2309.17453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.17453)
- **Differential Transformer** (Oct 2024) — difference of two softmax maps; needs 2x QK per head — [arXiv 2410.05258](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05258)
- **QK-norm, NoPE, partial RoPE, YaRN** — position/normalization variants that change prologue fusion only (NoPE in GLM-5.3-Flash MLA removes RoPE from the absorbed path) — [Megatron-LM PR #7054](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/7054)

### Inferences
- KV per token (BF16), computed from cited configs: gpt-oss-120b full layers = 18 layers x 2 x 8 KV heads x 64 dim x 2 B = ~36 KB/token (SWA layers capped at 128 tokens); Qwen3.6 = 10 layers x 2 x 2 x 256 x 2 B = ~20 KB/token. At 32K context these are ~1.2 GB and ~0.67 GB per sequence, i.e. ~1.2 ms and ~0.7 ms of 4090 DRAM time (~1 TB/s) per decode step. (gpt-oss layer/head counts from the model card; assumes 36 layers alternating.)
- For GLM-5.3-Flash the attention-side KV is tiny (11 MLA layers x ~512-dim latent), but 321B params will not fit in 24 GB VRAM + 64 GB RAM even at 4-bit — note for the coordinator.
- Absorbed-MLA decode on Ada must be a custom kernel (FlashInfer MLA FA2 path or TileLang/Triton); FlashMLA is unusable.

### Gaps
- No primary Google tech report found for Gemma 4 (head counts, exact KV sharing scheme). No official Qwen3.6 config fetched. GLM-5.3-Flash MLA latent dims not confirmed.

## Q3. Sparse attention (trained-native and training-free)

### Takeaway
Trained-native sparse attention (NSA, DSA, MoBA) changes the model and is required to serve models trained with it (GLM-5.3-Flash uses DSA-style top-2048 selection); training-free methods (Quest, MInference, DuoAttention, XAttention, SpargeAttn) are optional accelerators that only pay off at long context. On a 4090, sparse decode kernels for DSA must be written from scratch (FlashMLA sparse and cuDNN DSA indexer are Hopper/Blackwell-only).

### Cited Findings
- **NSA — Native Sparse Attention** (Feb 2025, DeepSeek; ACL 2025 best paper) — three branches (compressed block tokens, selected top-k blocks, sliding window) with learned gates; hardware-aligned block selection, GQA-shared selection. Reports up to ~9x fwd, 6x bwd, 11.6x decode at 64K. Kernels: Triton reference, fla NSA, TileLang — [arXiv 2502.11089](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11089); [fla README](https://github.com/fla-org/flash-linear-attention)
- **DSA — DeepSeek Sparse Attention** (DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp, 29 Sep 2025) — "lightning indexer" (tiny FP8 multi-head ReLU scorer over all previous tokens) + per-query top-k (2048) token selection, then MLA (MQA-mode) attention over only selected tokens: core attention O(L·k). FlashMLA sparse prefill/decode kernels (Hopper originally, now Blackwell/Ascend); NVIDIA cuDNN-frontend DSA module (experimental; indexer fwd/top-k SM100 only; FlashMLA forward not integrated). Indexer itself is still O(L^2) but cheap — [LMSYS day-0 blog](https://www.lmsys.org/blog/2025-09-29-deepseek-V32); [cuDNN DSA docs](https://docs.nvidia.com/deeplearning/cudnn/latest/fe-oss-apis/dsa.html); [FlashMLA repo](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/FlashMLA)
- **GLM-5.3-Flash "KPool DSA"** — DSA variant whose indexer scores pooled key blocks (kpool=4) and always keeps the tail, top-k 2048 — [Megatron-LM PR #7054](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/7054)
- **DeepSeek-V4 sparse MLA** — vLLM has a separate DeepSeek-V4 sparse-MLA backend — [vLLM docs](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/latest/api/vllm/models/deepseek_v4/sparse_mla/)
- **MoBA — Mixture of Block Attention** (Feb 2025, Moonshot) — MoE-style gating picks top-k KV blocks per query (mean-pooled block keys); deployed for Kimi long context; FlashMoBA backend in fla (Apr 2026) — [arXiv 2502.13189](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.13189); [fla README](https://github.com/fla-org/flash-linear-attention)
- **Quest** (Jun 2024) — query-aware page selection using per-page min/max key bounds; decode only, training-free; up to 7.03x self-attention speedup, 2.23x latency — [arXiv 2406.10774](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10774)
- **MInference 1.0** (Jul 2024, Microsoft) — offline-identified per-head patterns (A-shape, vertical-slash, block-sparse) for prefill; up to 10x prefill at 1M on A100 — [arXiv 2407.02490](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.02490)
- **DuoAttention** (Oct 2024) — split heads into retrieval (full KV) vs streaming (sink+window) heads; up to 2.55x memory reduction (MHA) — [arXiv 2410.10819](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10819)
- **XAttention / FlexPrefill / SpargeAttn** — training-free block-sparse prefill with antidiagonal scoring / adaptive patterns / online prediction — [XAttention arXiv 2503.16428](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.16428); [FlexPrefill arXiv 2502.20766](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.20766); [SpargeAttn arXiv 2502.18137](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.18137)
- **CPU-offloaded / retrieval attention** — RetrievalAttention, MagicPIG (LSH sampling on CPU), ShadowKV (low-rank K on GPU, V offloaded), InfiniGen; relevant if KV ever exceeds VRAM — [RetrievalAttention arXiv 2409.10516](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.10516); [MagicPIG arXiv 2410.16179](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.16179); [ShadowKV arXiv 2410.21465](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21465)

### Inferences
- DSA on Ada: indexer = small FP8/INT8 GEMM over all cached index-keys (Ada FP8 mma works) + top-k (radix select) + gather-based sparse MQA decode over 2048 latent rows. At short context (<2048 tokens) DSA degenerates to dense; gains appear only beyond a few K tokens.
- Training-free sparsity (Quest/MInference) is a quality risk and a low priority unless the target workload is >64K context.

### Gaps
- No Ada implementation of the lightning indexer or sparse MLA decode found. Exact GLM-5.3-Flash indexer head dims not found.

## Q4. Linear attention, state-space and recurrent layers, and their kernels

### Takeaway
Two of four targets are hybrids dominated by delta-rule recurrent layers (Qwen3.6: Gated DeltaNet 30/40; GLM-5.3-Flash: KDA 34/45). The reference kernels live in flash-linear-attention (fla, Triton, runs on sm_89); faster CUTLASS backends (FlashKDA) are SM90+. llama.cpp's GDN CUDA path has had performance/correctness issues, so this slot is a prime target for agent-written kernels: chunked (WY/UT-transform) prefill + fused recurrent decode + causal-conv1d.

### Cited Findings
- **Linear attention family list in fla** (as of Oct 2026): ABC, RetNet, HGRN/HGRN2, GLA, Based, Rebased, DeltaNet, RWKV6/7, LightNet, YOCO, Mamba2, GSA, MLA, Stick-Breaking, Samba, Gated DeltaNet, NSA, FoX, DeltaProduct, Rodimus, MesaNet, Comba, PaTH, MoM, Log-Linear Attention, DeltaFormer, KDA, MoBA, Mamba3 (2603.15569), Raven, Gated DeltaNet 2 (2605.22791), Wall Attention, Parallax (2605.29157), Preconditioned GDN/KDA (2604.21100), CAT (2511.05313), CyFA (2609.36259). Not usable on Ada — [FlashKDA docs index](https://docsearch.algolia.com/mcp/docs/repo/moonshotai/flashkda); [2nd Reddit snapshot](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/view/post/9510761)

- **llama.cpp GDN status** — issue reports a 16 GB GDN model running 4x slower than a 42 GB non-GDN model; Vulkan fell back to CPU; ROCm fused kernel no faster than CPU (suspected per-thread `float s[S_v]` register pressure); "chunked fused GDN path" (#20340) and a Metal GDN/KDA kernel (#20361) landed; a CUDA kernel `gated_delta_net.cu` exists; a single user reported garbage output on RTX 3090 for a later Qwen model with an old build — [llama.cpp issue #20354](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/issues/20354); [discussion #27164](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/discussions/27164); [HF ggml-gated-delta-net](https://huggingface.co/marcsun13/ggml-gated-delta-net)

### Inferences
- Kernel decomposition for GDN/KDA on Ada: (a) prefill = chunkwise algorithm (chunk 64): intra-chunk WY/UT transform (triangular solve) + inter-chunk state propagation, all mma.sync FP16/BF16 with FP32 state; (b) decode = fused recurrent step reading/writing the dk x dv FP32 state per head (memory-bound; state size per sequence is constant, tens of MB for a 30-layer model — assumption based on Qwen3-Next-like 128x128 heads, not verified for 3.6); (c) fuse conv1d-update + L2-norm of q/k + gate computation + output gate/RMSNorm into the decode step. KDA differs only by vector decay (extra elementwise work in the chunk kernel).
- At decode batch 1 the recurrent layers are cheap relative to MoE expert traffic; their main risk is launch overhead (many small kernels per layer) — CUDA graphs and fusion matter more than peak FLOPs.
- Register-pressure issue seen in llama.cpp ROCm (whole state row in registers) is a concrete pitfall for agent-written CUDA decode kernels: split dv across warps and keep state tiles in smem/registers balanced.

### Gaps
- No primary benchmark of fla Triton GDN/KDA kernels on RTX 4090. FlashQLA backend details (hardware support) not checked. Mamba-3 / GDN-2 / Preconditioned GDN specifics not read.

## Q5. What matters most on a single RTX 4090 with KV in VRAM and experts on CPU

### Takeaway
Attention is rarely the decode bottleneck in this setup; MoE expert weight movement (PCIe 3.0 / DDR4) dominates. The attention slot's priorities are: (1) correctness/coverage of every layer type on sm_89 (sinks+SWA, gated GQA head_dim 256, K=V global layers, absorbed NoPE-MLA + DSA, GDN, KDA) since most vendor kernels for these are Hopper/Blackwell-only; (2) low-overhead, CUDA-graph-able decode kernels with split-K and GQA packing; (3) quantized prefill attention (INT8/FP8, Sage-style) and FP8 KV for long contexts; (4) sparse/indexer kernels only for very long contexts.

### Cited Findings
- Hopper/Blackwell-only (unusable on sm_89): FA3 (sm90), FA4 (sm100), FlashMLA (SM100/103 now; sm90 at older commit), FlashKDA (SM90+), cuDNN DSA indexer (SM100), SageAttention3 FP4 (Blackwell) — [FA3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08608); [FA4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.05451.pdf); [FlashMLA](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/FlashMLA); [FlashKDA](https://docsearch.algolia.com/mcp/docs/repo/moonshotai/flashkda); [cuDNN DSA](https://docs.nvidia.com/deeplearning/cudnn/latest/fe-oss-apis/dsa.html); [SageAttention3](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.11594v3)
- Usable on sm_89: FA2, FlashInfer (Ada listed), xFormers, FlexAttention/Triton, fla (Triton; NVIDIA/AMD/Intel), SageAttention 1/2/2++ (Ada FP8 needs CUDA >= 12.4), ThunderKittens, TileLang, llama.cpp CUDA FA — [FA2](https://github.com/Dao-AILab/flash-attention); [FlashInfer 2nd](https://rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/flashinfer/en/latest/what-is-flashinfer.html); [fla](https://github.com/fla-org/flash-linear-attention); [SageAttention](https://github.com/thu-ml/SageAttention)
- RTX 4090 is reported to fall back to FA2 in vLLM/SGLang auto backend selection — [2nd: Spheron guide](https://www.spheron.network/blog/flashattention-4-blackwell-gpu-cloud-guide/)
- KV reductions in target models: Kimi-Linear-style 3:1 hybrid cuts KV ~75% (KDA) — [Kimi Linear](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26692); SWA layers bound KV at window size (gpt-oss 128, Gemma 4 1024) — [gpt-oss card](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10925); [aman.ai Gemma 4](https://aman.ai/primers/ai/gemma-4/)

### Inferences
- Rough decode budget (batch 1): attention KV read at 32K context is ~1 ms (gpt-oss ~36 KB/token, Qwen3.6 ~20 KB/token at BF16) whereas streaming ~several GB of active expert weights per token from DDR4 (~40-50 GB/s dual-channel) or PCIe 3.0 x16 (~13-16 GB/s) costs tens of ms. So attention optimizations move decode tok/s only a few percent below ~32K context; they matter more at 128K+ and in prefill. (Bandwidth figures are typical platform values, not sourced here.)
- Prefill (compute-bound) is where attention kernels pay: at long prompts attention FLOPs grow O(L^2) on full layers (only 18/36 gpt-oss, 10/40 Qwen3.6, 5/30 Gemma 4 layers), so INT8-QK/FP8-PV kernels (2-3x over FA2 on 4090 per Sage papers) are the biggest lever for time-to-first-token.
- FP8 (e4m3) KV cache halves KV bytes and is cheap to dequant on Ada; INT8/INT4 KV (llama.cpp q8_0/q4_0) further, with quality tradeoffs — belongs to the KV-cache slot but the dequant must be fused into the attention kernel.
- Recommended attention-slot variant set for the agent to search: {FA2-style prefill (BF16 / INT8-QK+FP8-PV / FP16-acc)} x {decode: split-K count, GQA packing, FP8 KV} x {masks: causal, SWA-128+sink, SWA-1024, tree}; {GDN/KDA: chunk size 16/32/64, fused recurrent decode, conv1d fusion}; {MLA absorbed decode + DSA indexer/top-k/gather} for GLM-5.3-Flash only if the model is runnable at all.
- Speculative decoding (MTP layer in GLM-5.3-Flash; EAGLE for others) converts decode into small-batch verification, which needs tree-mask attention and makes attention/MoE more GEMM-like — a cross-slot interaction worth flagging.

### Gaps
- No primary measurement of attention share of decode time for these specific models on a 4090 with CPU-offloaded experts.
- Gemma 4 global-layer head counts and KV sharing details unconfirmed; Qwen3.6 GDN state dimensions unconfirmed; GLM-5.3-Flash memory feasibility on 24 GB + 64 GB not checked against official quant sizes.

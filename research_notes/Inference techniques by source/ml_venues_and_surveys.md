# LLM inference-efficiency techniques from ML venues (2024–Oct 2026) and the major surveys

Scope: one row per paper/technique; 2024–2026; venues NeurIPS/ICML/ICLR/ACL/EMNLP/NAACL/COLM/AAAI plus the surveys and the awesome-lists that act as spines. Feeds the maxbox plugin ingestion queue.

## How to enumerate every 2024–2026 inference-efficiency technique, and what the spines say

### Takeaway
The awesome-lists and survey companion repos together enumerate roughly 1,500 inference-efficiency papers for 2024–2026; the ~430 rows below are the subset that is (a) a serving-time technique (not training-only), and (b) within the requested scope. Row-level claimed gains are reproduced only where a source in this pass stated them; everything else is marked `n/r` (not retrieved) rather than invented.

### Legend
- Preconditions: `TF` = training-free; `calib` = needs calibration data; `FT` = fine-tuning/retrofit training of the base model; `train-aux` = trains an auxiliary module (draft head, evictor, predictor) but not the base model; `arch` = must be chosen at pretraining time. Model-feature requirements are stated explicitly.
- Regime: `P` prefill, `D` decode, `P+D` both.
- Claimed gain / quality: `n/r` = not retrieved in this pass; `†` = recalled from the paper from memory, not re-verified here (listed again under Unverified).
- Venue shown as the awesome-list/leaderboard records it; `arXiv` = no venue recorded.

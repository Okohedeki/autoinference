# LLM-inference papers from systems venues, 2024 – Oct 2026 (enumeration for the maxbox ingestion queue)

Scope: OSDI, SOSP, ATC, EuroSys, ASPLOS, MLSys, NSDI, ISCA, MICRO, HPCA, PPoPP, SC, VLDB/FAST, plus arXiv systems preprints that engines cite. One row per paper/technique. Target machine for flags: RTX 4090 (sm_89, 24 GB) + AVX2 CPU + 64 GB DDR4 with CPU expert offload.

Method actually executed (40 tool calls): fetched the accepted-paper / technical-session pages for OSDI'24/'25/'26, ATC'24/'25, SOSP'24/'25, EuroSys'24/'25, ASPLOS'24/'26, MLSys'24 (proceedings.mlsys.org), MLSys'25 (proceedings.mlsys.org), MLSys'26 (papers.cool mirror), NSDI'25 (first half), and ran web searches for EuroSys'26, ASPLOS'25, ISCA/MICRO/HPCA, NSDI/SC/VLDB. Pages that failed: EuroSys'26 accepted list (404 / schedule only), ASPLOS'25 program (404; the /program/ URL redirects to 2026), ATC'26 (404), MLSys virtual pages (JS-rendered), dblp (bot-blocked). Rows whose venue could not be confirmed from a fetched list are tagged **[unverified]** in the paper column and collected again in the "Unverified items" section at the end.

Column legend. **regime**: DC = multi-GPU datacenter serving; 1GPU = single server GPU; CONSUMER = consumer GPU and/or CPU-GPU hybrid / offload; EDGE = phone/SoC/MCU; HW = new hardware (simulated). **lossless?**: Y = output distribution unchanged (pure systems); N = approximate/quantized; ~ = near-lossless per paper. **Flag** column shows "4090" when the paper directly targets or evaluates consumer-GPU / offload settings. Claimed gains are the headline numbers as stated in the paper abstract or venue listing; they are not independently verified.
